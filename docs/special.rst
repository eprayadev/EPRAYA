Special functions
=================

Here are some utilitary functions for the EPR cw analysis, from angle dependance to multiple energy diagrams.

   * - Name

   * - :doc:`epraya.Nrotate <Nrotate>`
     - Shows the variation of the resonant fields in relation to the rotations of the sample by its z axis.
   * - :doc:`epraya.Ori <Ori>`
     - Creates the energy diagrams for orientations parallel to the X, Y and Z axis.
   * - :doc:`epraya.Pot <Pot>`
     - Plotting for the Ori function energy diagrams. 
   * - :doc:`epraya.Spectre <Spectre>`
     - Plotting function for spectrum, energy diagram and angle variation.
